# The 9 Things the CrowdStrike Zscaler Talks Prove in 2026, and Why Your Security Stack Is About to Shrink

*Two rivals, one 265 billion dollar combination, and the end of buying security one tool at a time.*

![A security operations room with rows of servers and monitoring screens](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/beechrasta/medium-images/main/2026-11-22/cyber-ops-room.jpg)
*A security operations room. CrowdStrike and Zscaler want to sell you the whole room, not one screen of it. Photo: WebandNet*

Let's stop pretending your company buys cybersecurity.

It buys a dozen tools that barely talk to each other, then hires people to glue them together.

On Friday, November 20, **Bloomberg** reported **CrowdStrike** is in talks to buy **Zscaler**, and the deal would end that era for good, or collapse trying.

## 1. What actually happened on Friday

- **Bloomberg** reported the talks first, citing people familiar with the matter, and **Reuters** confirmed them with its own sources the same day
- The discussions are ongoing, and a combination could come together soon, but there is **no certainty** a deal happens at all
- Neither company commented. That silence is normal at this stage, and it tells you nothing either way

**Talks are not a deal. But the market reacted like the deal is already half done.**

*(Source: [Channel News Asia](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/business/crowdstrike-in-talks-acquire-zscaler-sources-say-6570666))*

## 2. The market voted in minutes

- **Zscaler** shares jumped roughly **17%** in after hours trading, to around **230 dollars**
- **CrowdStrike** fell roughly **8%**, to around **209 dollars**, because buyers always pay a premium and shareholders do the maths first
- Before the report, **CrowdStrike** was worth about **235 billion dollars**, after roughly doubling this year, and **Zscaler** about **32 billion dollars**. Together, about **265 billion dollars**

**When one stock jumps 17% and the other drops 8% on the same headline, the market has already decided who needs this deal more.**

*(Source: [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/174733-crowdstrike-in-talks-to-buy-zscaler-in-what-would-be-one-of-the-largest-/))*

## 3. What each side actually sells

- **CrowdStrike's Falcon** platform protects endpoints: laptops, servers, and mobile devices, using AI to spot and stop threats on the device itself
- **Zscaler** pioneered **zero trust** security, which verifies every user and every device before granting access to corporate apps and data, instead of trusting anything inside the network
- One guards the device. The other guards the connection. Together, they cover the two halves every company actually needs

**Endpoint plus network is the whole perimeter, and the perimeter is now the product.**

*(Source: [Security Boulevard](https://securityboulevard.com/2026/11/crowdstrike-in-talks-to-acquire-zscaler-in-potential-blockbuster-cybersecurity-deal-reports/))*

## 4. The gap this deal fills

- **CrowdStrike's** biggest weakness has always been network security, and **Zscaler** is exactly that missing piece
- Buying it would let **CrowdStrike** compete directly with platform providers like **Palo Alto Networks**, instead of selling a brilliant endpoint tool into someone else's platform
- The scale is new for **CrowdStrike**: its largest deal to date was the **627.9 million dollar** purchase of identity provider **SGNL** in February, and earlier this month it agreed to buy AI security startups **Fiddler AI** and **Dreadnode**

<u>A 32 billion dollar acquisition is not a product purchase. It is a company deciding what it wants to be.</u>

*(Source: [InfoRisk Today](https://www.inforisktoday.com/blogs/crowdstrike-eyeing-buy-zero-trust-titan-zscaler-p-4264))*

## 5. The real enemy is Palo Alto

- **Palo Alto Networks** has spent 2026 buying everything around security: **Chronosphere** for 3 billion dollars, **Koi** for 231 million, **Portkey** for 117 million, **Embrace Mobile** for 325 million, and **Console Systems** for 500 million
- **Palo Alto** is pushing beyond security into the general AI space, while **CrowdStrike** is doubling down on core cyber functions
- A combined **CrowdStrike** and **Zscaler** would rival **Palo Alto** in both breadth and valuation, which is the entire point

**This is not two companies merging. It is two platform strategies colliding, and only one gets to be the default.**

*(Source: [InfoRisk Today](https://www.inforisktoday.com/blogs/crowdstrike-eyeing-buy-zero-trust-titan-zscaler-p-4264))*

## 6. 2026 is the year cybersecurity consolidated

- This year already saw the two largest cybersecurity acquisitions in history close: **Google's 32 billion dollar** purchase of **Wiz**, and **Palo Alto's CyberArk** acquisition
- A **CrowdStrike** and **Zscaler** combination would rank alongside those, among the largest cyber deals ever, next to **Cisco's** roughly **28 billion dollar Splunk** deal
- The driver is simple: enterprise customers want to **reduce the number of security vendors** they manage, and buy multiple products through a single relationship

**Customers stopped buying best of breed a while ago. They buy fewest vendors, and the vendors heard them.**

*(Source: [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/174733-crowdstrike-in-talks-to-buy-zscaler-in-what-would-be-one-of-the-largest-/))*

## 7. AI agents forced this deal

- Businesses are deploying AI applications and **autonomous agents** everywhere, and every agent is a new identity, a new set of permissions, and a new attack surface
- Securing those agents is now the battleground, and neither endpoint tools nor network tools can cover it alone
- **Zscaler** felt this fear first: its shares fell sharply earlier this year in a broader software selloff as investors weighed what AI would do to software, then rebounded

**AI agents do not just use your network. They are your network now, and they need guarding like employees with keys.**

*(Source: [Channel News Asia](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/business/crowdstrike-in-talks-acquire-zscaler-sources-say-6570666))*

## 8. The numbers that make it possible

- **CrowdStrike's** stock has more than doubled in value this year, which gives it the currency to buy, while **Zscaler** is down roughly **12%** in 2026, which makes it buyable
- **Zscaler's** fate rests with its largest holders: founder **Ajay Mangal** with 18.2%, chief executive **Jay Chaudhry** with 16.8%, and **The Vanguard Group** with 7.1%
- **Chaudhry** founded **Zscaler** in 2007 and has led it for 21 years, and selling to **CrowdStrike** would keep the firm under a product focused leader in **George Kurtz**

**A founder ready to exit, a buyer with a doubled stock, and a market begging for platforms. That is how 32 billion dollar deals get born.**

*(Source: [InfoRisk Today](https://www.inforisktoday.com/blogs/crowdstrike-eyeing-buy-zero-trust-titan-zscaler-p-4264))*

## 9. How to start: what to do if you buy or build security

- **If you buy security:** count your vendors today, then ask each one what a platform price looks like, because consolidation means your renewal quotes are about to change
- **If you build AI agents:** treat every agent as a new employee with network access, and secure it with zero trust thinking from day one, not as an afterthought
- **If you invest:** remember talks collapse, and the 8% drop in **CrowdStrike** is the market pricing in exactly that risk
- **If you work in security:** learn the platform story, because point tool expertise is worth less every quarter this consolidation runs

**The stack is shrinking whether you plan for it or not. Plan for it.**

*(Source: [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/11/20/crowd-pleaser-crowdstrike-wants-to-buy-zscaler/))*

## The real pattern

Security used to be sold as a collection of clever tools, each guarding one door. Then the doors multiplied, AI agents started walking through them on their own, and customers realised they were paying twelve vendors to guard one house. The pattern is not that big companies are buying small ones. It is that the unit of sale changed from a tool to a platform, and the companies that cannot offer the whole platform are becoming someone else's acquisition.

## Final thoughts

Your security stack is about to get smaller, cheaper to manage, and harder to escape.

**CrowdStrike** and **Zscaler** may or may not become one company. But the direction is already decided: fewer vendors, bigger platforms, and AI agents as the thing everyone is really guarding now.
