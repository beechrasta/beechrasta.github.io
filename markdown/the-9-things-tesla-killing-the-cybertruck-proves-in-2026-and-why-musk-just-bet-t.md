# The 9 Things Tesla Killing the Cybertruck Proves in 2026, and Why Musk Just Bet the Company on Robots

The most hyped truck of the decade is dead, and the factory that built it is about to build robots instead.

![A black Cybertruck parked in a parking lot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/beechrasta/medium-images/main/2026-11-07/cybertruck-hero.jpg)
*A black Cybertruck in a parking lot. The truck that was supposed to sell 200,000 a year. Photo by Varun Palaniappan on Unsplash.*

Let's stop pretending this is about a truck. The **Cybertruck** was never really a vehicle, it was a bet that **Tesla** could sell a stainless steel triangle to pickup drivers. That bet just lost **$1.9 billion**.

On Friday, **Elon Musk** announced on **X** that **Tesla** will wind down **Cybertruck** production at **Gigafactory Texas** around the middle of next year, to make room for increased **Cybercab** production. The factory floor is going to a robotaxi with no steering wheel, and to a humanoid robot.

This article is not a eulogy for a weird truck. It is about what happens when the world's most valuable car company stops being a car company.

## 1. The truck died by spreadsheet, not by opinion

- **Musk** had said **Tesla** aimed to sell 200,000 **Cybertrucks** a year. It never came close.
- In Q3 2026, **Tesla** delivered only 8,300 "other models", the category that includes the **Cybertruck**. In the same quarter, the **Model 3** and **Model Y** combined for 478,000 deliveries.
- Total **Cybertruck** sales since late 2023 sit at roughly 70,000, against installed tooling for 125,000 a year.

**The truck sold about a tenth of what the factory was built to make.**

*(Source: [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/06/tesla-to-wind-down-cybertruck-production-as-elon-musk-focuses-on-robotics-and-ai))*

## 2. Musk's own math is brutal

- **Tesla** spent **$2 billion** on **Cybertruck** research and development.
- It made just **$100 million** in gross profit from sales.
- Net project loss: **$1.9 billion**, by **Musk's** own breakdown.

**Nobody killed the Cybertruck. The numbers did.**

*(Source: [CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/11/06/tesla-cybertruck-production-halted-elon-musk-2026-elections/))*

## 3. The factory floor is the real story

- The **Cybertruck's** space at **Gigafactory Texas** goes to the **Cybercab**, **Tesla's** self-driving taxi.
- The same factory will also build **Optimus**, **Tesla's** humanoid robot.
- **Musk** did not say the **Cybertruck** would be built anywhere else, and moving it would need significant new investment in specialised tooling.

**Factory floor space is the most valuable real estate an automaker has, and Tesla just gave it to robots.**

*(Source: [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/06/tesla-to-wind-down-cybertruck-production-as-elon-musk-focuses-on-robotics-and-ai))*

## 4. The replacement has no steering wheel

- The **Cybercab** seats two passengers and has no steering wheel at all.
- **Tesla** has started offering paid trips in it in limited parts of **Austin, Texas**.
- **Musk** has said material revenue from the **Cybercab** is unlikely before at least 2027.

**Tesla is swapping a truck people can buy today for a taxi that barely exists yet. That is the whole bet.**

*(Source: [The Outpost](https://theoutpost.ai/news-story/tesla-ends-cybertruck-production-after-selling-just-70-000-units-pivots-to-ai-and-robotics-33200/))*

## 5. Tesla stopped being a car company a while ago

- **Tesla** also stopped production this year of the **Model S** and **Model X**, its premium saloon and SUV, amid declining sales.
- **Musk** has pinned **Tesla's** hopes on robotics, artificial intelligence and an autonomous-driving fleet, not on a steady stream of new models.
- The **Semi** lorry is still ramping up, and a road version of the **Roadster** is aimed for 2028, but analysts do not expect it to move profits much.

**Every other carmaker sells cars to fund the future. Tesla is selling the future to fund the future.**

*(Source: [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/06/tesla-to-wind-down-cybertruck-production-as-elon-musk-focuses-on-robotics-and-ai))*

## 6. The truck never even got to Europe

- The **Cybertruck** never went on sale in Europe because it did not meet the required safety standards.
- The $39,900 base price **Musk** promised in 2019 never materialised, and neither did the 500-mile range.
- Prices climbed instead: the Premium AWD rose from $84,990 to $92,490, and the **Cyberbeast** from $99,990 to $102,490.

**A pickup that cannot be sold in Europe, cannot hit its launch price, and cannot hit its launch range is not a product. It is a demo.**

*(Source: [Torque News](https://www.torquenews.com/1/5-reasons-cybertruck-didn-t-catch-it-s-legacy-and-what-tesla-dropping-it-means-owners))*

## 7. The timing is not an accident

- Republican candidates **Musk** backed with **$446 million** in donations lost in the US midterm elections.
- The paper value of **Musk's** fortune fell by **$113 billion** as investors questioned whether a Democrat-led Congress could slow the AI boom.
- **Tesla** shares fell 11% on Thursday, while **SpaceX** dropped 9%.

**Musk announced the death of his most famous product at the end of his worst week of the year. He buried the news in his own bad news.**

*(Source: [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/06/tesla-to-wind-down-cybertruck-production-as-elon-musk-focuses-on-robotics-and-ai))*

## 8. What this means for the 70,000 owners

- Production ending does not change existing warranty terms.
- Owners should keep service records, watch resale values closely, and ask **Tesla** for clear answers on long-term parts support.
- By late 2025, about one in five new **Cybertrucks** registered in the US had not been bought by the public at all, but purchased as internal fleet vehicles by **Musk's** other ventures.

**Seventy thousand people bought the future, and the future just got discontinued. Their trucks still work, but they now own a collector's item that depreciates like a gadget.**

*(Source: [AutoGuide](https://www.autoguide.com/auto/manufacturers/tesla-manufacturers/the-tesla-cybertruck-is-officially-dead-44641762))*

## 9. Musk saw this coming, and said so out loud

- In a 2023 earnings call, before sales began, **Musk** said: "We dug our own grave with **Cybertruck**," citing enormous challenges in ramping production and making money from it.
- He was right, and he shipped it anyway.
- The 2019 reveal promised over a million pre-orders and 250,000 units a year. The reality was 70,000 trucks in three years.

<u>The most honest thing Musk ever said about the Cybertruck was before anyone could buy one.</u>

*(Source: [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/06/tesla-to-wind-down-cybertruck-production-as-elon-musk-focuses-on-robotics-and-ai))*

## How to read Tesla now

- **If you are an investor:** stop valuing **Tesla** like a carmaker. Its story is robotaxis, robots, and AI, and none of those have proven revenue yet.
- **If you are a car buyer:** the **Model 3** and **Model Y** are the company now. Everything else is a side quest.
- **If you are a developer or robotics student:** the **Cybercab** and **Optimus** are where the hiring and the hard problems live. The truck is history, the robot is the job.
- **If you own a Cybertruck:** keep your paperwork, and enjoy owning the most recognisable discontinued car of the decade.

## The real pattern

Companies do not pivot when they are winning. Tesla killed its most famous product, in its worst week, to clear floor space for machines that do not have steering wheels or drivers, because the car business stopped being the point. The Cybertruck was the last Tesla built to impress humans. Everything after it is built to replace them.

## Final thoughts

The **Cybertruck** was a mirror, and it showed us exactly what we wanted to see: a future that looked like science fiction. The future **Tesla** is actually building looks nothing like that. It has two seats, no steering wheel, and no driver. Whether that future arrives on time is the only question left, and it is worth a lot more than $1.9 billion.
